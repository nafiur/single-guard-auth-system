Advanced Thermal-Break Technology Integration

EBON doors and windows utilize high-grade polyamide glass fiber reinforced strips (PA66GF25) which act as severe barrier walls inside the hollow structure of extruded aluminum alloy, stopping active thermal flow. Accompanied by triple seal barriers, double co-extruded EPDM gaskets, and specialized gas cavity seals, our thermal break system ensures maximum environmental shielding properties.

Furthermore, our 3i-Low-E triple-glazing architecture blocks 97% of harmful solar UV waves, significantly reducing building HVAC energy metrics to contribute to sustainable ecological building accreditations like LEED or BREEAM.
